POWERTRAIN

S58 3.0L M TwinPower Turbo Inline-6

Developed directly from GT3 racing experience, the S58 features a closed-deck crankcase, forged crankshaft, 3D-printed cylinder head core for optimized cooling channels, and twin mono-scroll turbochargers spinning up to 7,200 RPM.

350 Bar Fuel Injection 7,200 RPM Redline Up to 543 HP Factory Output
DRIVETRAIN

M xDrive with Active M Differential

Unlike conventional all-wheel-drive systems, M xDrive maintains a distinct rear-wheel bias. Drivers can switch seamlessly between 4WD, 4WD Sport, and pure 2WD mode with Dynamic Stability Control deactivated for pure drifting dynamics.

0–100% Variable Torque Split 3 Modes 4WD / 4WD Sport / 2WD
ELECTRIFICATION

Gen6 eDrive & Neue Klasse Architecture

BMW's next-generation cylindrical battery cells increase energy density by over 20% while cutting charging times by 30%. Paired with an 800-volt architecture and four centralized "Superbrain" computers, Neue Klasse unifies drivetrain and chassis control into milliseconds.
